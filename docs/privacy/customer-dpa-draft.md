# Business customer data processing agreement — internal draft

Not ready for execution. The customer order, verified legal entities, authorized subprocessor schedule, processing locations, transfer arrangements, retention decisions and security evidence must be completed first. This draft must not be published as an executed contract.

## Parties and scope

For personal data that a business customer determines should be processed in its projects, the intended arrangement is customer as controller and SR IT support services as processor. Account administration, security and statutory financial records require a separate role assessment; do not label every activity processor-only.

Subject matter: hosting project state and performing requested AI-assisted build operations. Duration: provision of the service followed by approved return/deletion and legally required retention. Nature: receipt, storage, model submission, generation, execution in sandboxes, retrieval and deletion. Data types may include customer-supplied text, code and project files. Data subjects depend on the customer's content, potentially including staff, users or clients. Special-category data requires an explicit assessment and agreed restrictions.

## Proposed processor commitments

Process customer personal data only on documented lawful instructions, including instructions about international transfers. Notify the customer where an instruction is considered unlawful, unless legally prohibited. Ensure authorized personnel are bound by confidentiality and limited to necessary access.

Apply and maintain appropriate technical and organizational safeguards. The security schedule should identify verified controls for authentication, project ownership, encryption, privileged access, sandbox isolation, metering, recovery, incident response and deletion. Code changes alone are not evidence of deployed controls; attach the staging/production verification record before signing.

Use subprocessors only under the agreed authorization process and equivalent data-protection obligations. Maintain the actual vendor/entity/location schedule and an advance-change notification and objection process agreed with the customer. Do not invent a notice period or claim an account-level DPA has already been signed.

Assist the customer with applicable rights requests, security duties, breach assessment, DPIAs and regulator consultation, taking account of the processing and available information. Inform the customer of a personal data breach without undue delay, provide available facts in stages and cooperate with containment and investigation. A processor notification must not wait for a controller's regulatory reporting deadline.

At the end of services, return or delete customer personal data at the customer's choice, including copies, except where law requires retention. Document any such exception, restrict retained data and complete the approved backup procedure. Maintain sufficient information to demonstrate compliance and permit appropriate audits and inspections under agreed confidentiality and security arrangements.

These provisions are based on the processor-contract requirements in [GDPR Article 28](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj/eng). They are a drafting basis, not a representation that all necessary schedules or procedures currently exist.
