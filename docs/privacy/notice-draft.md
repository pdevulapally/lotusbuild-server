Do not claim that LotusBuild never processes sensitive information: customers could include it in prompts or projects. Minimize collection, explain restrictions, and review special-category processing before permitting it.

## Sharing and children

Public project sharing is an intended feature. Public content can be copied by recipients; access controls cannot recall those copies. Explain the audience before an owner enables sharing and provide revocation. Current provider ingress must be tested before describing previews as private.

The owner selected parent-owned accounts with linked access for under-16 users. A child link, verified email or age declaration is not automatically proof of adult identity, parental responsibility or a particular statutory consent standard. The final notice must explain what the parent can see and control in language children can understand. Users aged 16 and 17 still need appropriate child protections. [ICO Children's Code guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/childrens-information/childrens-code-guidance-and-resources/faqs-on-the-15-standards-of-the-children-s-code/).

## Retention and rights

Retention periods are pending approval in `retention-draft.md`. Do not publish the proposed periods as a live promise until deletion and backup-expiry tests demonstrate them.

People may contact info@sritsupportservices.co.uk about access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, objection or applicable consent withdrawal. The authenticated privacy-request endpoint records requests; it does not itself complete an export or account erasure. Requests from people unable to sign in must also be accepted through the contact channel. Determine the applicable rights, verify identity proportionately, protect others' information and communicate any justified limitation.

Privacy complaints can be sent to the same address. Acknowledge them within 30 days, investigate and communicate the result without undue delay. Explain how to complain to the ICO or the appropriate EU supervisory authority. [ICO's June 2026 complaints requirements](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2026/06/new-data-protection-complaints-law-now-in-force/).
